Incidents

Something went wrong operationally: an alarm nobody received, a broken audit chain, a failed backup, or anything an operator records by hand. Each incident carries its impact, the cause found and the corrective and preventive action (CAPA); it cannot be closed without them. The system opens its own incidents and tells administrators by e-mail and browser notification.
