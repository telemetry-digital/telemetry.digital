Roles and permissions

Who is allowed what

Built-in roles are fixed. Organization roles (marked) can be edited below; a user's permissions are the union of all their roles.

Server administration

The built-in role server_admin administers the whole server: every organization, server settings, the VPN of the whole server, backups, updates and the licence (permission system.admin). org_admin administers this organization only. Only a server administrator may grant or remove server_admin, always with a reason; the last server administrator cannot lose it, and nobody can remove their own administration roles or disable themselves.
