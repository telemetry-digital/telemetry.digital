Remote cameras (relay) — security

Camera

A relay at the remote site (ctrl32-telemetry camera-relay) reads the local camera and sends the video over an encrypted connection (RTSPS) that it opens itself — no open port and no VPN at the site. After saving you get the camera's secret and the relay configuration, once.
RTSP with H.264, H.265 or MJPEG, or an HTTP MJPEG address. User name and password may be part of the URL; they are stored separately and the password is encrypted.
For a camera behind a relay: its ONVIF address in its own network, e.g. http://192.168.0.21:2020/onvif/device_service for TP-Link Tapo. The server reaches it through the relay, which needs onvif = "http://192.168.0.21:2020" for this camera in relay.toml. User name and password above are the camera's account (Tapo: the camera account from the Tapo app); the password is stored encrypted.
Events that start recording
Disk space for sound per camera and day: G.711 (Tapo) about 1.4 GB, AAC 0.2–0.7 GB; the picture takes 10–40 GB.
Recording sound (conversations) is regulated more strictly than pictures: switch it on only where it is lawful and people are informed (a sign). The camera may send AAC, Opus or G.711 (TP-Link Tapo and most cameras). Hearing and exporting sound needs the permission video.audio and is recorded in the audit trail. A relay forwards sound only with audio = true.
The server compares the pictures of an MJPEG stream (the sub-stream first), or the camera's JPEG snapshot once a second — it does not decode H.264/H.265. Motion zones (below, after saving) limit where motion counts. Events look like the camera's own: they show on walls and plans, start recording on events and flows.
Draw over the picture:
Drag over the picture to cover an area (a neighbour's window, a cash desk, a public street). The areas are covered wherever the picture is shown; the recording stays complete and only people with the permission to see unmasked pictures can see or export it. Masks set in the camera itself keep the areas out of the recording.

Relay for the remote camera

The secret is shown only now. Copy the configuration to the relay at the remote site; a lost secret can only be replaced by a new one.

1. Save this configuration as relay.toml and fill in the local address of the camera (and its password).

2. On a computer at the site that reaches the camera, run the installer next to the file:

Linux / Raspberry Pi
curl -fsSL https://portal.telemetry.digital/install-relay.sh -o install-relay.sh
sudo bash install-relay.sh --config relay.toml
Windows (PowerShell as administrator)
irm https://portal.telemetry.digital/install-relay.ps1 -OutFile install-relay.ps1
.\install-relay.ps1 -Config .\relay.toml

The installer tests the connection to the server, the camera and the secret, then runs the relay as a service that starts with the computer. Delete the copied file afterwards.
