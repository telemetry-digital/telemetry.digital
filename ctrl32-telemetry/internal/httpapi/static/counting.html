Areas and entrances

An area is a building, a car park or a zone. People and vehicles are counted at its entrances; the occupancy is arrivals minus departures, never below zero, reset every day at the chosen time. Every count is stored as a datastream of the area's and the entrance's asset, so dashboards, period tables, alarm rules, flows and exports work with it.

Counting with cameras

Detector installation

The server finds people and vehicles in the camera's picture and counts them when they cross a line you draw. The picture is analysed on this server and never stored; only the numbers are. Off until you switch it on for a camera.

Counting with sensors

Light barriers, turnstiles, door counters and any device that reports passages over MQTT or HTTP (the device protocol) count for an entrance: choose the datastream of passages in and of passages out. A value is either the number of passages since the last message, or a running total.

Correct occupancy

For example after counting the people in the building by hand. The value and the reason are written to the counting log and the audit trail.

Capacity alarm

An alarm rule on the occupancy datastream of the area: a warning and an action limit. Alarms are notified like any other alarm (escalation policies, e-mail, SMS, Web Push) and can start flows.

Export

A period table of the area's datastreams: arrivals and departures as sums, the occupancy as its maximum. The same table is available as a dashboard widget and as a scheduled report by e-mail.
