Displays

A display is a screen at a wall — a PC, a mini-PC or a smart TV with a browser — that shows dashboards, process screens and video walls in turn, without anyone signed in. It shows only what it is given, changes nothing and can be disconnected at any time. Cameras with an event can enlarge themselves on it, and an operator can send a camera to it.

Kiosk PCs

The Windows computers behind the displays. A small agent on each PC reports its state and carries out what is asked here: restart the browsers, reboot, update its scripts, and a screenshot when it is allowed for the PC. The PC connects to this server; nothing connects to the PC.
