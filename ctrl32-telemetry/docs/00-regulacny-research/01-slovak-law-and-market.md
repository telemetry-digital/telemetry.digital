# Slovak legal framework (ŠÚKL / GDP / GLP) and the EU competitive landscape for pharmaceutical temperature monitoring

> **Research date: 21 September 2026.** Key correction to the assignment brief up front: the brief suggested *vyhláška 129/2012* implements správna veľkodistribučná prax. It does not. **128/2012 Z. z.** = správna výrobná prax + správna veľkodistribučná prax (GMP + GDP). **129/2012 Z. z.** = správna lekárenská prax (GPP). This is verified below.
>
> **The single most commercially important finding of this research is in Part A, Q2/Q3: vyhláška č. 419/2025 Z. z. amended 129/2012 so that from 1 July 2026 every Slovak pharmacy must measure temperature with a *calibrated* instrument capable of *automatic* recording at intervals of *at most two hours*, and must keep daily records for 12 months. That mandate is already in force as of this research date and creates a compulsory, deadline-driven market of ~2,000+ Slovak pharmacies.**

## Q1. Zákon č. 362/2011 Z. z. — which § govern wholesale distribution, storage conditions, and the duty to follow GDP/GMP

### Takeaway
The Act itself sets only the framework duties: § 15 obliges manufacturers to follow správna výrobná prax, § 18 obliges wholesale distributors to follow správna veľkodistribučná prax and to store medicines under prescribed conditions, and § 23 obliges pharmacy-care providers to follow správna lekárenská prax. The operative technical detail (temperature, recording, retention) is delegated to the two implementing vyhlášky, not found in the Act.

### Cited Findings
- The Act is *Zákon č. 362/2011 Z. z. o liekoch a zdravotníckych pomôckach a o zmene a doplnení niektorých zákonov*, consolidated text on Slov-Lex — [Slov-Lex 362/2011](https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky-fe/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2011/362); static consolidated version as at 31.01.2025 — [static.slov-lex.sk 362/2011](https://static.slov-lex.sk/static/SK/ZZ/2011/362/20250131.html)
- **§ 18** is headed *Povinnosti držiteľa povolenia na veľkodistribúciu liekov*. Its ods. 1 obliges the holder to comply with **"dodržiavanie správnej veľkodistribučnej praxe"**, to ensure **"uchovávanie liekov v podmienkach podľa predpisu … vrátane teploty"**, and imposes documentation/evidence duties with retention **"najmenej päť rokov"** — [static.slov-lex.sk 362/2011](https://static.slov-lex.sk/static/SK/ZZ/2011/362/20250131.html); consolidated text also at [zakonypreludi.sk 362/2011](https://www.zakonypreludi.sk/zz/2011-362)
- **§ 15 ods. 1 písm. a)** imposes on the manufacturing-licence holder **"dodržiavanie správnej výrobnej praxe"** — [static.slov-lex.sk 362/2011](https://static.slov-lex.sk/static/SK/ZZ/2011/362/20250131.html)
- **§ 23 ods. 1 písm. a)** imposes on the holder of a licence to provide lekárenská starostlivosť **"dodržiavanie správnej lekárenskej praxe"** — [static.slov-lex.sk 362/2011](https://static.slov-lex.sk/static/SK/ZZ/2011/362/20250131.html)
- A secondary Slovak legal commentary source confirms § 18 as the seat of *Povinnosti držiteľa povolenia na veľkodistribúciu liekov* — [Lewik legal dictionary](https://www.lewik.org/term/2498/povinnosti-drzitela-povolenia-na-velkodistribuciu-liekov-zakon-o-liekoch-a-zdravotnickych-pomockach/)

### Inferences
- For a vendor's sales argument, § 18 (distributors) and § 23 (pharmacies) are the correct "hook" paragraphs to cite in a proposal, but the *technical* specification a customer will be inspected against lives in vyhlášky 128/2012 and 129/2012. A sales one-pager should cite both layers.
- Because the Act delegates the detail, a change in customer obligations can arrive via a ministerial decree alone (as it did in December 2025) without any parliamentary amendment — meaning the regulatory trigger for demand can move fast and with little warning.

### Gaps
- **The exact písmeno-level verbatim Slovak wording of § 18 ods. 1 could not be captured reliably.** Slov-Lex serves its consolidated texts through a JavaScript front end and blocked direct retrieval; `curl` to `static.slov-lex.sk` was refused by the sandbox egress proxy (`connect_rejected`), and the WebFetch summariser returned paraphrase plus only short fragments in quotation marks. The § numbers and the substance (GDP duty, storage incl. temperature, five-year retention) are corroborated across two independent sources, **but the report writer should not present the § 18 fragments above as exact verbatim quotations without a final check against Slov-Lex.** The fragments are best treated as accurate paraphrase.
- § 18 ods. 1 uses lettered sub-clauses; I could not confirm which specific letter carries the storage/temperature duty (the fetch suggested písm. b) for GDP and písm. c) bod 1 for storage, but this was not independently corroborated).

## Q2. Which vyhláška implements správna veľkodistribučná prax and správna lekárenská prax — current numbers, temperature and record-keeping provisions

### Takeaway
Two separate decrees, both from 2012: **128/2012 Z. z.** (GMP + GDP) and **129/2012 Z. z.** (GPP). 128/2012 appears unamended since it took effect on 15 May 2012 and still requires only *regular* recording plus indication/alarm devices. 129/2012 was substantially amended by **vyhláška č. 419/2025 Z. z.**, which from 1 July 2026 imposes calibrated instruments with automatic recording at ≤2-hour intervals on pharmacies.

### Cited Findings

**The GDP/GMP decree — 128/2012 Z. z.**
- Full title: *Vyhláška Ministerstva zdravotníctva Slovenskej republiky č. 128/2012 Z. z. o požiadavkách na správnu výrobnú prax a požiadavkách na správnu veľkodistribučnú prax* — [Slov-Lex 128/2012](https://slov-lex.sk/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2012/128); [ASPI consolidated text](https://www.aspi.sk/products/lawText/1/77336/0/2/vyhlaska-c-128-2012-zz-o-poziadavkach-na-spravnu-vyrobnu-prax-a-poziadavkach-na-spravnu-velkodistribucnu-prax); [EPI 128/2012](https://www.epi.sk/zz/2012-128)
- **Správna veľkodistribučná prax is regulated in DRUHÁ ČASŤ, § 12–19** — [static.slov-lex.sk 128/2012](https://static.slov-lex.sk/static/SK/ZZ/2012/128/vyhlasene_znenie.html)
- **§ 15 ods. 1** (*Uchovávanie liekov, liečiv, pomocných látok a obalov*): *"Lieky, liečivá, pomocné látky a obaly sa uchovávajú oddelene. V tom istom priestore na uchovávanie nemožno uchovávať iný tovar."* The same odsek requires storage under manufacturer-specified conditions so as to prevent damage by heat or humidity, and states that **temperature and humidity are *regularly* recorded, checked and evaluated** (*"Teplota a vlhkosť sa pravidelne zaznamenávajú, kontrolujú a vyhodnocujú."*) — [static.slov-lex.sk 128/2012](https://static.slov-lex.sk/static/SK/ZZ/2012/128/vyhlasene_znenie.html)
- **§ 15 ods. 2** (verbatim): *"Ak sa na uchovávanie liekov vyžadujú osobitné podmienky na teplotu a vlhkosť priestoru na uchovávanie, priestory na uchovávanie sa vybavia zariadeniami indikujúcimi a signalizujúcimi nedodržanie požadovanej teploty a vlhkosti."* — [static.slov-lex.sk 128/2012](https://static.slov-lex.sk/static/SK/ZZ/2012/128/vyhlasene_znenie.html)
- **§ 18 ods. 1** (*Dokumentácia*, verbatim): *"O veľkodistribúcii liekov sa vedie dokumentácia tak, aby bolo možné sledovať a kontrolovať všetky dôležité úkony veľkodistribúcie liekov. Dokumentácia sa uchováva najmenej päť rokov."* — [static.slov-lex.sk 128/2012](https://static.slov-lex.sk/static/SK/ZZ/2012/128/vyhlasene_znenie.html); [zakonypreludi 128/2012](https://www.zakonypreludi.sk/zz/2012-128)
- **§ 18 ods. 5**: documentation must be stored so that persons exercising štátny dozor can inspect it (*"sa ukladá tak, aby do nej mohli nazrieť osoby vykonávajúce štátny dozor"*) — [static.slov-lex.sk 128/2012](https://static.slov-lex.sk/static/SK/ZZ/2012/128/vyhlasene_znenie.html)
- **§ 5 ods. 4** (manufacturing side): *"Zariadenia, stroje a prístroje používané pri výrobe lieku a jeho kontrole sa pravidelne overujú, kalibrujú a validujú."* — [zakonypreludi 128/2012](https://www.zakonypreludi.sk/zz/2012-128)
- **§ 13** (distribution premises) refers to **"kalibrované teplomery a vlhkomery"** — [zakonypreludi 128/2012](https://www.zakonypreludi.sk/zz/2012-128)
- The zakonypreludi consolidated view of 128/2012 shows **only one version, effective 15.05.2012, with no listed novely** — [zakonypreludi 128/2012](https://www.zakonypreludi.sk/zz/2012-128)

**The pharmacy decree — 129/2012 Z. z., as amended by 419/2025 Z. z.**
- Full title: *Vyhláška Ministerstva zdravotníctva Slovenskej republiky z 21. marca 2012 o požiadavkách na správnu lekárenskú prax* — [ŠÚKL-hosted PDF of 129/2012](https://www.sukl.sk/buxus/docs/Inspekcia/Legislativa/vyhlaska_129_2012.pdf); [Slov-Lex 129/2012](https://www.slov-lex.sk/ezbierky/pravne-predpisy/SK/ZZ/2012/129/)
- **In the ORIGINAL 2012 text**, § 30 ods. 8 and § 31 ods. 3 each read simply: *"Teplota a vlhkosť sa denne počas prevádzky zaznamenávajú, kontrolujú a vyhodnocujú."* — [ŠÚKL-hosted PDF of 129/2012](https://www.sukl.sk/buxus/docs/Inspekcia/Legislativa/vyhlaska_129_2012.pdf)
- **In the CURRENT text, § 30 ods. 8** reads: *"Lieky, liečivá a pomocné látky sa uchovávajú za podmienok určených držiteľom registrácie lieku, výrobcom liečiv a pomocných látok, Európskym liekopisom alebo Slovenským farmaceutickým kódexom tak, aby sa zabránilo akémukoľvek ich poškodeniu teplom alebo vlhkom. **Teplota sa meria pomocou kalibrovaného meradla, ktoré umožňuje meranie a automatickú evidenciu teploty najmenej v dvojhodinových intervaloch. Denné záznamy o teplote sa uchovávajú 12 mesiacov.**"* — [zakonypreludi 129/2012](https://www.zakonypreludi.sk/zz/2012-129)
- **§ 31 ods. 3** (medical devices, dietetic foods, supplementary range) carries the identical sentence: *"Teplota sa meria pomocou kalibrovaného meradla, ktoré umožňuje meranie a automatickú evidenciu teploty najmenej v dvojhodinových intervaloch. Denné záznamy o teplote sa uchovávajú 12 mesiacov."* — [zakonypreludi 129/2012](https://www.zakonypreludi.sk/zz/2012-129)
- The amending instrument is **vyhláška č. 419/2025 Z. z.**, *ktorou sa mení a dopĺňa vyhláška Ministerstva zdravotníctva Slovenskej republiky č. 129/2012 Z. z. o požiadavkách na správnu lekárenskú prax*, **published 15 December 2025**, with effect **1 January 2026 for most points and 1 July 2026 for the temperature points** — [zakonypreludi 419/2025](https://www.zakonypreludi.sk/zz/2025-419); effective date corroborated at [zakonypreludi 129/2012](https://www.zakonypreludi.sk/zz/2012-129)
- The temperature changes are **body 32 (§ 30 ods. 8) and 33 (§ 31 ods. 3)** of 419/2025, and the requirement for a *"kalibrované meradlo umožňujúce meranie a automatickú evidenciu teploty"* at ≤2-hour intervals is **repeated through numerous annex amendments (body 39, 40, 47, 50, 52, 53, 55–59)** covering different pharmacy departments/room types — [zakonypreludi 419/2025](https://www.zakonypreludi.sk/zz/2025-419)
- Independent trade-press confirmation that this is a new obligation: *"Automatické zaznamenávanie teploty v lekárňach ako nová povinnosť od 1. júla 2026"*, reporting that "using calibrated thermometers with automatic temperature recording will become a mandatory part of pharmacy operations in Slovakia", replacing reliance on manual reading of simple thermometers with no retrospective verification — [mediPRÁVNIK](https://www.medipravnik.sk/pravne-clanky/automaticke-zaznamenavanie-teploty-v-lekarnach-ako-nova-povinnost-od-1-jula-2026-2091/)

### Inferences
- **The legal asymmetry is the commercial story.** Pharmacies (129/2012, as amended) now have a *prescriptive, testable* rule — calibrated, automatic, ≤2 h, 12-month retention — whereas wholesale distributors (128/2012) still live under a *vague* rule ("pravidelne", plus indicating/signalling devices). A prescriptive rule is far easier to sell against: the buyer cannot argue that a wall thermometer and a paper sheet suffice.
- The requirement is written as a property of the *meradlo* ("kalibrované meradlo, ktoré umožňuje meranie a automatickú evidenciu"), not of a system or of software. Read literally it mandates an automatic-recording calibrated data logger; it does **not** on its face mandate cloud software, alarms, audit trail, or Annex 11 computerised-system validation. A minimal compliant product is therefore a calibrated logger plus a 12-month record archive — which caps what a vendor can credibly *require* a pharmacy to buy, while leaving alarms/remote access/reporting as genuine upsells rather than compliance necessities.
- The 12-month retention for pharmacy daily temperature records versus 5 years for distributor documentation means a pharmacy-focused product needs only a modest data-retention commitment, whereas a distributor-focused product must guarantee 5-year retrievability — a meaningful difference in hosting cost and in contractual/exit terms.
- Because 419/2025 repeats the requirement across many annex points for individual room types, a pharmacy with several separate storage rooms plus a fridge plausibly needs **multiple** measuring points, not one. Sizing proposals per room rather than per site is both more accurate and commercially favourable.

### Gaps
- I could not retrieve the full official Slov-Lex consolidated text of 129/2012 post-amendment (JavaScript front end; static host blocked by proxy). The § 30 ods. 8 / § 31 ods. 3 wording above comes from zakonypreludi.sk's consolidated text and is corroborated by the separate 419/2025 page, but has not been checked against the official Zbierka text.
- I could **not positively confirm** that 128/2012 has never been amended. The zakonypreludi page displayed no novely, which is suggestive but is an absence of evidence; the ASPI and Slov-Lex pages were not read closely enough on this point. **The report should state that 128/2012 appears unamended but flag it as requiring confirmation**, especially since a parallel GDP-side amendment would materially change the distributor opportunity.
- The full body of 419/2025's other changes (points 1–31, 34–38 etc.) was not reviewed; there may be further relevant obligations (e.g. on documentation or premises) beyond temperature.
- Whether any transitional provision softens the 1 July 2026 date for existing pharmacies was not established.

## Q3. Record retention periods imposed on wholesale distributors and pharmacies

### Takeaway
Three different retention periods apply depending on who you are and what the record is: **5 years** for wholesale distribution documentation, **12 months** for pharmacy daily temperature records, and **3 years** for vaccine cold-chain records under Ministry of Health expert guidance.

### Cited Findings
- **Wholesale distributors — 5 years.** Vyhláška 128/2012 § 18 ods. 1: *"Dokumentácia sa uchováva najmenej päť rokov."* — [static.slov-lex.sk 128/2012](https://static.slov-lex.sk/static/SK/ZZ/2012/128/vyhlasene_znenie.html)
- **Wholesale distributors — 5 years, statutory level.** Zákon 362/2011 § 18 also requires retention of acquisition/supply records *"najmenej päť rokov"* — [static.slov-lex.sk 362/2011](https://static.slov-lex.sk/static/SK/ZZ/2011/362/20250131.html)
- **Pharmacies — 12 months for daily temperature records.** Vyhláška 129/2012 § 30 ods. 8 and § 31 ods. 3 (as amended by 419/2025, in force 1.7.2026): *"Denné záznamy o teplote sa uchovávajú 12 mesiacov."* — [zakonypreludi 129/2012](https://www.zakonypreludi.sk/zz/2012-129)
- **Vaccines — 3 years.** Slovak professional guidance on vaccine storage states written cold-chain records must be archived for three years, citing *Odborné usmernenie MZ SR* — [Solen, *Uchovávanie a distribúcia očkovacích látok*](https://www.solen.sk/storage/file/article/Lancova.pdf)
- Wholesale distribution records must also be stored so that state-supervision officers can consult them — vyhláška 128/2012 § 18 ods. 5 — [static.slov-lex.sk 128/2012](https://static.slov-lex.sk/static/SK/ZZ/2012/128/vyhlasene_znenie.html)

### Inferences
- A single product sold to both segments must default to the **longest applicable** period (5 years) or make retention configurable per customer type; selling a 12-month-retention SaaS tier to a distributor would leave them non-compliant.
- The 3-year vaccine rule sits *above* the 12-month pharmacy floor for the same physical fridge. A pharmacy that stores vaccines therefore effectively needs 3-year retention for that fridge. This is a concrete, defensible reason to sell the higher retention tier to any pharmacy doing vaccination — and a detail most generic HACCP loggers will not have considered.
- Because the periods conflict, "retention configurable and documented per storage location" is itself a differentiator worth naming explicitly in product documentation.

### Gaps
- The precise identifier, date and current validity of the *Odborné usmernenie MZ SR* on vaccination cold chain was not established (the Solen article cites it generically as published in the Ministry's Vestník). Its 3-year rule should be verified before being relied on in customer-facing material, as the article may predate current guidance.
- Whether the 5-year distributor retention explicitly covers *temperature* records (as opposed to transactional records) is not stated in terms; § 18 ods. 1 speaks of documentation of "všetky dôležité úkony veľkodistribúcie", which is broad but not explicit on environmental monitoring data.

## Q4. ŠÚKL — published inspection information, common findings, and guidance on temperature monitoring or mapping

### Takeaway
ŠÚKL publishes its inspection framework, a list of GDP certificate holders, a list of documents required for entry inspection, and — most usefully — a public list of deficiencies found in pharmacy inspections, plus annual-report statistics. Temperature and calibration deficiencies feature prominently for both distributors and pharmacies. **I found no Slovak ŠÚKL guidance document on temperature mapping**; the equivalent guidance exists only on the Czech side (SÚKL's DIS-15).

### Cited Findings
- ŠÚKL's 2024 annual report records **73 inspections of veľkodistribútori** and **478 inspections at providers of lekárenská starostlivosť** — [ŠÚKL Výročná správa 2024](https://www.sukl.sk/buxus/docs/rozhodnutia/Vyrocna_sprava_2024_final.pdf)
- Frequent deficiencies at **veľkodistribútori** per the 2024 annual report include: *"nedostatočne vykonaný teplotný monitoring priestorov"*, *"chýbajúci teplotný monitoring počas prepravy"*, and *"chýbajúce kalibrácie kľúčových meradiel"* — [ŠÚKL Výročná správa 2024](https://www.sukl.sk/buxus/docs/rozhodnutia/Vyrocna_sprava_2024_final.pdf)
- Frequent deficiencies at **lekárne** per the same report include: *"nedostatočná kontrola/udržiavanie teplotných podmienok v rámci priestorov lekárne"*, *"chýbajúce kalibrované teplomery a vlhkomery, resp. chýbajúca záznamová dokumentácia k monitorovaniu teplôt"*, and *"nedostatočný dohľad nad prepravnými podmienkami v rámci internetového výdaja"* — [ŠÚKL Výročná správa 2024](https://www.sukl.sk/buxus/docs/rozhodnutia/Vyrocna_sprava_2024_final.pdf)
- ŠÚKL maintains a dedicated public page, *Zistené nedostatky pri priebežných inšpekciách v lekárňach*, listing concrete findings including: *"nie je teplomer v chladničke"*; *"teplota a vlhkosť sa neevidujú denne"*; *"teplomery nie sú kalibrované"*; *"teplomery a vlhkomery nie sú vo všetkých miestnostiach, kde sa skladujú lieky"*; *"na chladničke nie je zoznam liekov, nie sú vyznačené doporučené teploty uchovávania"* — [ŠÚKL, Zistené nedostatky pri priebežných inšpekciách v lekárňach](https://www.sukl.sk/hlavna-stranka/slovenska-verzia/inspekcia/lekarenstvo/zistene-nedostatky-pri-priebeznych-inspekciach-v-lekarnach?page_id=1724)
- **Caveat on that page's currency:** it cites vyhláška č. 198/2001 and č. 274/1998 as the governing decrees, i.e. the *predecessors* of 129/2012 and 128/2012 — [ŠÚKL, Zistené nedostatky](https://www.sukl.sk/hlavna-stranka/slovenska-verzia/inspekcia/lekarenstvo/zistene-nedostatky-pri-priebeznych-inspekciach-v-lekarnach?page_id=1724). That 198/2001 was the older správna lekárenská prax decree is confirmed at [AUTORA 198/2001](https://www.autora.sk/zz/2001/198/).
- ŠÚKL publishes a **Zoznam vydaných osvedčení o dodržiavaní správnej veľkodistribučnej praxe** (list of issued GDP certificates) — [ŠÚKL GDP certificate list](https://www.sukl.sk/hlavna-stranka/slovenska-verzia/inspekcia/distribucia/zoznam-vydany-osvedceni-o-dodrziavani-spravnej-velkodistribucnej-praxe)
- ŠÚKL publishes the documents required for an entry inspection of a distributor: application form TL ODL 025 *"Žiadosť o vydanie posudku – veľkodistribúcia"*, document checklists for new companies / new activities / changed premises, a *"Prevádzkový poriadok spoločnosti – odporúčaný obsah"* and a *"Hygienicko-sanitačný poriadok – odporúčaný obsah"*. Inspections are scheduled within 30 days of a complete application and findings issued within 30 days after inspection. **The page does not explicitly list temperature mapping, calibration, qualification or validation documents** — [ŠÚKL, Doklady potrebné pre vykonanie vstupnej inšpekcie](https://sukl.sk/hlavna-stranka/slovenska-verzia/inspekcia/distribucia/doklady-potrebne-pre-vykonanie-vstupnej-inspekcie?page_id=1248)
- ŠÚKL maintains a legislation page for the inspection division — [ŠÚKL Legislatíva](https://www.sukl.sk/hlavna-stranka/slovenska-verzia/inspekcia/legislativa?page_id=900)
- ŠÚKL has published training material on *Klasifikácia nedostatkov v kvalite a postup pri sťahovaní nevyhovujúcich liekov z trhu* — [ŠÚKL PDF](https://www.sukl.sk/buxus/docs/O_nas/Vzdelavanie/Klasifikacia_nedostatkov_v_kvalite_a_postup_pri_stahovani_nevyhovujucich_liekov_z_trhu.pdf)
- **Czech comparator:** the Czech SÚKL publishes a dedicated temperature guideline, **DIS-15 verze 4, *Sledování a kontrola teploty při skladování a přepravě léčiv***, updated February 2025 — [DIS-15 v4 PDF](https://sukl.gov.cz/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/DIS-15_verze4_Sledovani-a-kontrola-teploty-pri-skladovani-a-preprave-leciv.pdf); [SÚKL CZ landing page](https://sukl.gov.cz/pokyny-ustavu/pokyny-distribuce-leciv/dis-15-verze-4/)

### Inferences
- **Temperature and calibration are among ŠÚKL's named recurring findings for both segments.** This is the strongest possible sales evidence a small Slovak vendor can use, because it is the regulator's own published words rather than vendor marketing. A one-page "what ŠÚKL actually writes down" leaflet quoting these findings verbatim would likely outperform any technical brochure.
- The absence of a Slovak ŠÚKL mapping/monitoring guideline, contrasted with the Czech DIS-15, is itself an opportunity: Slovak customers have no authoritative national how-to, so a vendor who produces a clear Slovak-language methodology note (aligned to EU GDP 2013/C 343/01 and, by analogy, DIS-15) fills a genuine documentation vacuum and earns advisory credibility. It is also a risk to manage honestly — such a note must be presented as the vendor's interpretation, not as ŠÚKL guidance.
- The ŠÚKL deficiencies page citing repealed decrees (198/2001, 274/1998) suggests it has not been revised in many years. Findings remain indicative of inspector behaviour, but the report writer should not present it as current regulatory guidance. It also hints that Slovak pharmacies may be receiving little updated official communication — increasing the value of a vendor who explains the 2026 change clearly.
- 478 pharmacy inspections in 2024 against a pharmacy population of roughly two thousand implies a meaningful annual chance of inspection for any given pharmacy, which makes the compliance purchase feel non-theoretical to the buyer.

### Gaps
- The annual-report figures (73 / 478) were extracted from a PDF by a summarising fetch, not read directly from the page. They should be spot-checked before being quoted as statistics in a published report.
- **No ŠÚKL inspection checklist for temperature monitoring or mapping was found**, nor any ŠÚKL position on whether continuous electronic monitoring is expected of distributors. Searches for "ŠÚKL usmernenie mapovanie teplôt" returned only Czech and commercial results.
- Sanctions/fines for temperature deficiencies were behind a paywall on mediPRÁVNIK and were not established.
- 2025 annual-report figures were not located; the most recent retrieved report is for 2024.
- The total number of registered pharmacies and licensed wholesale distributors in Slovakia (i.e. the addressable market size) was not established from a primary source.

## Q5. Requirements for lekárne specifically — GPP and pharmacy fridge / vaccine monitoring

### Takeaway
From 1 July 2026 every Slovak pharmacy storage location — including fridges and each room where medicines are stored — must have a calibrated instrument with automatic recording at ≤2-hour intervals and 12-month record retention. Vaccine-specific guidance layers on a 2–8 °C range, no-freezing rule, daily (ideally twice-daily) records including non-working days, min–max capability, and 3-year archiving.

### Cited Findings
- **Core obligation (from 1.7.2026):** vyhláška 129/2012 § 30 ods. 8 / § 31 ods. 3 as amended — calibrated instrument, automatic recording at least every two hours, daily records kept 12 months — [zakonypreludi 129/2012](https://www.zakonypreludi.sk/zz/2012-129); [vyhláška 419/2025](https://www.zakonypreludi.sk/zz/2025-419)
- **Scope across rooms:** 419/2025 repeats the calibrated-automatic-recording requirement across multiple annex points (body 39, 40, 47, 50, 52, 53, 55–59) for different pharmacy departments — [zakonypreludi 419/2025](https://www.zakonypreludi.sk/zz/2025-419)
- **ŠÚKL already penalises the manual status quo:** published findings include *"nie je teplomer v chladničke"*, *"teplomery nie sú kalibrované"*, *"teplota a vlhkosť sa neevidujú denne"*, and *"teplomery a vlhkomery nie sú vo všetkých miestnostiach, kde sa skladujú lieky"* — [ŠÚKL, Zistené nedostatky](https://www.sukl.sk/hlavna-stranka/slovenska-verzia/inspekcia/lekarenstvo/zistene-nedostatky-pri-priebeznych-inspekciach-v-lekarnach?page_id=1724)
- **Vaccines, 2–8 °C and no freezing:** *"Teploty pod bodom mrazu môžu nezvratne redukovať účinnosť vakcín"*; DTP combinations must not be stored below +2 °C — [Solen, Uchovávanie a distribúcia očkovacích látok](https://www.solen.sk/storage/file/article/Lancova.pdf)
- **Vaccines, recording frequency:** *"Teplota v chladničke musí byť denne písomne zaznamenaná (vrátane dní pracovného voľna)"* — daily, including non-working days, ideally twice per day at the start and end of shifts — [Solen](https://www.solen.sk/storage/file/article/Lancova.pdf)
- **Vaccines, sensor placement and type:** thermometer placed in the centre of the storage space; min–max thermometers are optimal because they *"monitorujú skutočnú teplotu, minimálnu aj maximálnu teplotu"* — [Solen](https://www.solen.sk/storage/file/article/Lancova.pdf)
- **Vaccines, record content:** records must include date, time, temperature reading, the staff member's name, and any corrective actions taken; archived three years — [Solen](https://www.solen.sk/storage/file/article/Lancova.pdf)
- Regulatory owner for the vaccine cold chain is the **Úrad verejného zdravotníctva SR**, with the requirements traced to an *Odborné usmernenie MZ SR* published in the Ministry's bulletin — [Solen](https://www.solen.sk/storage/file/article/Lancova.pdf)
- General Slovak public-health context on vaccination is published by ÚVZ SR — [ÚVZ SR](https://www.uvzsr.sk/web/uvz)

### Inferences
- The "including non-working days" rule is decisive for product design: it cannot be satisfied by a staff member reading a dial, which is precisely why automatic logging was mandated. A vendor should lead with this, because it converts the purchase from "nice to have" to "the only way to comply on a Sunday".
- The vaccine guidance's requirement that records carry the *responsible person's name and corrective action* is not satisfied by a bare logger export. A product that produces a monthly Slovak-language report with space for sign-off and deviation comments matches the actual inspection artefact the pharmacist must present — a small feature with disproportionate perceived value.
- Per-site sensor count for a typical pharmacy is plausibly 2–4 (at minimum one fridge probe plus one per medicine-storage room), which sets the realistic deal size rather than a single-sensor sale.
- The requirement is on the *meradlo* being calibrated, so calibration certificate management — not the electronics — is the recurring obligation and the natural recurring-revenue hook.

### Gaps
- The exact identifier and current text of the ÚVZ SR / MZ SR *odborné usmernenie* on the vaccination cold chain was not retrieved; all vaccine specifics above come from a secondary professional-journal article ([Solen](https://www.solen.sk/storage/file/article/Lancova.pdf)) which may not reflect current guidance.
- Whether the vaccine guidance's twice-daily manual recording survives alongside (or is superseded by) the new automatic-recording rule is unresolved — the two instruments have different legal status (decree vs. expert guidance) and I found no source reconciling them.
- No source was found specifying required *accuracy* (e.g. ±0.5 °C) or required alarm thresholds for pharmacy instruments under Slovak law.

## Q6. Who may calibrate — SNAS accreditation, and is a monitoring sensor an "určené meradlo" under zákon 157/2018 Z. z.?

### Takeaway
The evidence indicates that temperature sensors/thermometers used for medicine storage are **not** "určené meradlá" (legally controlled instruments requiring periodic *overenie* by an authorised body), so **traceable/accredited calibration suffices** — and the pharmacy decree independently requires the instrument to be "kalibrované", not "overené". Accredited ISO/IEC 17025 calibration is commercially available in Slovakia at modest cost.

### Cited Findings
- *Určené meradlá* are defined by **§ 11 zákona 157/2018 Z. z.** as *"meradlá, ktoré sa bez vykonania povinnej metrologickej kontroly alebo posúdenia zhody nesmú uviesť na trh a používať"*; the classification depends on use — payments, protection of health/safety/property/environment, consumer packaging, and similar public-interest measurements — [ÚNMS SR, FAQ metrológia](https://www.normoff.gov.sk/stranka/247/faq-metrologia/)
- Calibration vs. verification, per ÚNMS: *"Cieľom kalibrácie je vytvorenie vzťahu medzi hodnotami indikovanými meradlom a hodnotami reprezentovanými etalónom"*, whereas verification determines *"či hodnoty indikované určeným meradlom vyhovujú z hľadiska najväčších dovolených chýb"* — [ÚNMS SR, FAQ metrológia](https://www.normoff.gov.sk/stranka/247/faq-metrologia/)
- **General thermometers are not listed among určené meradlá** in the ÚNMS FAQ; heat meters (*merače tepla*) are, when installed before apartments — [ÚNMS SR, FAQ metrológia](https://www.normoff.gov.sk/stranka/247/faq-metrologia/)
- The list of určené meradlá and their metrological control is set out in **vyhláška č. 161/2019 Z. z. o meradlách a metrologickej kontrole** (Annex 1 sets verification intervals) — [ASPI, vyhláška 161/2019](https://www.aspi.sk/products/lawText/1/92023/0/2/vyhlaska-c-161-2019-zz-o-meradlach-a-metrologickej-kontrole)
- Users of určené meradlá must maintain valid verification under § 16 ods. 1 and order subsequent verification *"v lehote 30 dní pred skončením platnosti overenia"* — [ÚNMS SR, FAQ metrológia](https://www.normoff.gov.sk/stranka/247/faq-metrologia/); user obligations summarised commercially at [NajlacnejšieMeradlá](https://najlacnejsiemeradla.sk/poradca/povinnosti-pouzivatelov-urcenych-meradiel/)
- **The pharmacy decree uses the word "kalibrovaného meradla"**, not "overeného" — vyhláška 129/2012 § 30 ods. 8 — [zakonypreludi 129/2012](https://www.zakonypreludi.sk/zz/2012-129). Likewise 128/2012 § 13 refers to *"kalibrované teplomery a vlhkomery"* and § 5 ods. 4 to equipment being *"pravidelne overujú, kalibrujú a validujú"* — [zakonypreludi 128/2012](https://www.zakonypreludi.sk/zz/2012-128)
- **SNAS accredits calibration laboratories in Slovakia** and publishes the accreditation scope framework — [SNAS, Kalibračné laboratóriá](https://www.snas.sk/akreditacia/kalibracne-laboratoria); [SNAS, Oblasť a rozsah akreditácie (PDF)](https://www.snas.sk/storage/app/uploads/public/690/b52/44e/690b5244e1cd6236226642.pdf); methodological guideline [MSA-L/07](https://www.snas.sk/storage/app/uploads/public/61a/3a6/ca1/61a3a6ca14371704010175.pdf)
- Commercial accredited thermometer calibration is available in Slovakia, e.g. ChiranaLAB/metrologia.sk advertising *"Kalibrácia teplomerov – Akreditované merania"* — [metrologia.sk](https://metrologia.sk/sluzby/teplomery-a-vlhkomery/) (page itself was not retrievable: robots.txt fetch failure); Elektronika.sk advertising an accredited laboratory for temperature instruments — [Elektronika.sk](https://www.elektronika.sk/kalibracia-teplomerov-a-meradiel-teploty)
- **KALIST AKL** states: *"Laboratórium teploty KALIST AKL je akreditované Českým inštitútom pre akreditáciu"* — i.e. accredited by the **Czech** accreditation body (ČIA), not SNAS — and calibrates thermometers from −196 °C to 1100 °C — [KALIST AKL, Kalibrácia teplomerov](https://www.kalistakl.sk/kalibracia-teplomerov)
- Metershop.sk states its accredited calibrations comply with **zákon 157/2018** — [Metershop, Kalibrácia teplomerov](https://metershop.sk/sluzby/kalibracia-teplomerov/)

### Inferences
- **Traceable/accredited calibration almost certainly suffices; periodic legal verification (overenie) is almost certainly not required** for medicine-storage temperature sensors. Two independent strands support this: the ÚNMS FAQ does not list thermometers as určené meradlá, and the health decrees themselves consistently use the language of *kalibrácia* rather than *overenie*. The decree wording is the stronger of the two, because it is what an inspector reads.
- ISO/IEC 17025 accreditation from **any** EA-member national body (ČIA, DAkkS, SNAS…) is mutually recognised in the EU, as KALIST's ČIA-accredited laboratory selling into Slovakia illustrates. A Slovak sole trader therefore does **not** need to build or buy a SNAS accreditation; he can subcontract accredited calibration and resell it. This materially lowers the barrier to entry — accreditation is the expensive part of this business and it can be rented.
- Conversely, the sole trader must be careful **not** to claim to be an accredited calibration laboratory himself. The honest and still-marketable position is "sensors supplied with an ISO/IEC 17025 accredited calibration certificate from [named laboratory], with recalibration managed on your behalf".
- Because the legal hook is the *calibrated certificate*, the defensible recurring revenue is certificate lifecycle management (tracking expiry, swapping sensors, filing certificates for inspection), not the hardware.

### Gaps
- **I did not read Annex 1 of vyhláška 161/2019 directly.** The conclusion that thermometers are not určené meradlá rests on the ÚNMS FAQ's silence plus the decrees' wording, which is strong but not conclusive. This should be verified against the annex text before being asserted flatly in customer-facing material, and the report should carry the caveat.
- The § of zákon 157/2018 governing calibration and traceability obligations for non-designated instruments was not quoted verbatim.
- **No Slovak legal source specifies a mandatory recalibration interval** for pharmacy/warehouse thermometers. The commonly marketed annual interval appears to be commercial convention rather than a legal requirement — I found no provision imposing it. This is an important honesty point: a vendor claiming "the law requires annual recalibration" would be overstating.
- A current list of SNAS-accredited temperature calibration laboratories in Slovakia (names and accreditation numbers) was not extracted; the SNAS register pages were identified but not enumerated.

## Q9. Typical cost of a temperature mapping study in the EU

### Takeaway
**Not published by any vendor I could find.** Mapping is sold as a bespoke service across the board. What *is* publicly established is the technical scope that drives the cost — sensor grid, 48–72 h duration, and summer/winter repetition.

### Cited Findings
- Eupry, asked directly for mapping pricing, publishes only: *"The services come with flexible pricing tailored to your specific requirements."* — [Eupry mapping pricing](https://eupry.com/temperature-monitoring/mapping-price/)
- Mapping is legally anchored: *"EU GDP (2013/C 343/01) and WHO TRS 961 both require mapping before first use of a warehouse or controlled area, and risk-based requalification when conditions change."* — [Eupry GDP mapping guide](https://eupry.com/gdp/gdp-mapping/)
- **Sensor density:** WHO TRS 961 suggests an Electronic Data Logging Monitor grid of **5–10 m spacing** in small/medium facilities, adapted to geometry and HVAC complexity, prioritising extremes, airflow influences and vertical variation — [Eupry GDP mapping guide](https://eupry.com/gdp/gdp-mapping/)
- **Duration:** *"often 48–72 hours for warehouses"*, plus challenge tests, with duration chosen to capture normal operational patterns — [Eupry GDP mapping guide](https://eupry.com/gdp/gdp-mapping/)
- **Seasonality:** *"Map in winter and summer if ambient conditions materially affect the space; justify with data or past trends."* — [Eupry GDP mapping guide](https://eupry.com/gdp/gdp-mapping/)
- Delivery models available in the market: short-term sensor rental (3–4 months), long-term rental (1–2 years), or full on-site service with accredited start calibration, validation software, remote support and optional protocol/report writing — [Eupry mapping pricing](https://eupry.com/temperature-monitoring/mapping-price/)
- The Czech regulator's DIS-15 v4 is the nearest authoritative regional methodology for temperature monitoring and control in storage and transport — [SÚKL CZ DIS-15 v4 (PDF)](https://sukl.gov.cz/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/DIS-15_verze4_Sledovani-a-kontrola-teploty-pri-skladovani-a-preprave-leciv.pdf)
- Regional mapping-only specialists exist in Czechia (Kalist, Kaplan Elektro, LogHub CZ), indicating mapping is a viable standalone service line — [Kalist](https://www.kalist.cz/teplotni-mapy); [Kaplan](https://www.kaplan.cz/menu/teplotni-mapy); [LogHub CZ](https://loghub.cz/teplotni-mapovani/)

### Inferences
- The 48–72 h duration and 5–10 m grid mean a mid-sized Slovak distributor warehouse plausibly needs on the order of tens of loggers for a few days, twice a year. Since calibrated loggers cost €70–€150 each locally (Q8), the *equipment* cost of entering mapping services is modest — likely low thousands of euros — while the billable deliverable is the protocol and report. This is an unusually favourable cost structure for a sole trader.
- The universal absence of published mapping pricing means customers cannot benchmark, which cuts both ways: margins are protected, but buyers are wary. A published fixed-price mapping offer for small Slovak sites would be genuinely novel.
- Mapping is a *distributor/warehouse* service, not a pharmacy service — it is the natural second product once a pharmacy monitoring business is established, and it targets the segment (128/2012) where the legal rule is vaguer and the buyer more sophisticated.

### Gaps
- **No EUR figure for a mapping study was obtained from any source.** The report must state plainly that EU mapping pricing is not published, rather than estimate one.
- Whether ŠÚKL expects mapping of *pharmacy* fridges (as opposed to warehouses) is not established; the Czech Kalist offers fridge/freezer mapping commercially, but no Slovak regulatory expectation was found.

## Q10. How vendors word Annex 11 / Part 11 compliance claims

### Takeaway
Vendors consistently use hedged, support-oriented language — "supports", "provides the basis to assess", "documentation packages to support" — rather than asserting that the customer is thereby compliant. The clear industry convention is that the *vendor* supplies capability and documentation; the *customer* owns validation.

### Cited Findings
- **ELPRO, overall statement:** *"ELPRO supports EU GMP Annex 11 and 21 CFR Part 11 requirements through a combination of secure system design, controlled access, traceable records, and documented validation support."* — [ELPRO, Documentation and Computerized Systems Regulations](https://www.elpro.com/en/learn/documentation-computerized-regulations)
- **ELPRO, scope framing:** systems *"meet European Union GMP Annex 11 and U.S. Food and Drug Administration 21 CFR Part 11 requirements"* in GxP storage — [ELPRO](https://www.elpro.com/en/learn/documentation-computerized-regulations)
- **ELPRO, audit trail:** *"Audit trails support data integrity by showing who did what, when, and what changed."* — [ELPRO](https://www.elpro.com/en/learn/documentation-computerized-regulations)
- **ELPRO, electronic signatures:** *"Expects secure, controlled use of electronic signatures where applied."* — [ELPRO](https://www.elpro.com/en/learn/documentation-computerized-regulations)
- **ELPRO, validation division of responsibility:** *"validated system use in regulated environments by providing the basis to assess"* — [ELPRO](https://www.elpro.com/en/learn/documentation-computerized-regulations)
- **ELPRO, supplier documentation:** *"ELPRO provides structured documentation packages to support implementation,"* — [ELPRO](https://www.elpro.com/en/learn/documentation-computerized-regulations)
- **ELPRO, product-page claim (stronger wording):** *"Fully compliant with FDA 21 CFR Part 11 and GAMP® 5 standards"* — [ELPRO pharmacy monitoring](https://www.elpro.com/en/pharmacy-temperature-monitoring)
- **Vaisala** substantiates its claims by publishing an actual **161-page IQ/OQ protocol sample** for viewLinc rather than by asserting compliance in prose — [Vaisala viewLinc IQ/OQ sample (PDF)](https://www.vaisala.com/sites/default/files/documents/VIM-G-Vaisala-viewLinc-5_0-IQOQ-SAMPLE.pdf)
- **Eupry** packages the supplier-audit response as a product feature, listing a **"quality questionnaire"** in its Enterprise tier — [Eupry pricing](https://eupry.com/temperature-monitoring/monitoring-price/)

### Inferences
- The gap between ELPRO's careful explainer page ("supports… provides the basis to assess") and its marketing page ("Fully compliant") is instructive: the defensible wording lives in the technical documentation, the aggressive wording in sales copy. A small vendor should adopt the *defensible* register throughout, because a sole trader has no legal department to absorb an overclaim and because a ŠÚKL inspector challenging a bold claim would damage the customer relationship irreparably.
- The reusable template for a small vendor's compliance statement is therefore: *[Product] supports the requirements of [EU GMP Annex 11 / vyhláška 129/2012 § 30 ods. 8] through [named technical features], and [vendor] supplies [calibration certificates / IQ-OQ documentation / supplier questionnaire responses] to support the customer's own validation. Responsibility for validation of the system in its intended use remains with the customer.*
- **For the Slovak market specifically, the highest-value claim is not Annex 11 or Part 11 at all — it is a clause-by-clause statement of conformity to vyhláška 129/2012 § 30 ods. 8 / § 31 ods. 3** (calibrated instrument, automatic recording ≤2 h, 12-month retention). No competitor found publishes such a mapping. That document is cheap to produce and directly answers the question a pharmacist will actually be asked by an inspector.
- Responding to supplier questionnaires is treated as chargeable enterprise work by incumbents; offering it free and in Slovak is a low-cost trust signal for a small supplier.

### Gaps
- I could not retrieve a full, verbatim vendor **compliance statement document** or a completed **supplier questionnaire response** — only the paraphrased and short-quoted wording above from ELPRO's explainer page. The fragments were returned by a summarising fetch and several were truncated with character counts, so they should be re-verified against the source before being reproduced as exact quotations.
- No Vaisala, Testo, Comet or LogHub compliance-statement wording was captured.
- No example of how any vendor words conformity to *Slovak* decrees was found — consistent with the inference that none does.

## Q11. Is there a market gap a small local supplier can exploit?

### Takeaway
Yes, and it is unusually well-defined: a statutory deadline (1 July 2026) has just created compulsory demand among small Slovak pharmacies; the global vendors are architected for enterprise scale and frame compliance in US terms; no competitor publishes prices or a Slovak-decree conformity statement. The main constraint is that LogHub, a Slovak manufacturer with 400+ installations, is already marketing on exactly this legislation.

### Cited Findings
- **A hard, dated statutory trigger exists**: calibrated instrument with automatic recording at ≤2-hour intervals, daily records kept 12 months, from 1 July 2026 — [zakonypreludi 129/2012](https://www.zakonypreludi.sk/zz/2012-129); [vyhláška 419/2025](https://www.zakonypreludi.sk/zz/2025-419); trade-press framing as a new obligation at [mediPRÁVNIK](https://www.medipravnik.sk/pravne-clanky/automaticke-zaznamenavanie-teploty-v-lekarnach-ako-nova-povinnost-od-1-jula-2026-2091/)
- **The regulator already writes up exactly these failures**: *"teplomery nie sú kalibrované"*, *"teplota a vlhkosť sa neevidujú denne"*, *"nie je teplomer v chladničke"* — [ŠÚKL, Zistené nedostatky](https://www.sukl.sk/hlavna-stranka/slovenska-verzia/inspekcia/lekarenstvo/zistene-nedostatky-pri-priebeznych-inspekciach-v-lekarnach?page_id=1724) — and at distributor level *"chýbajúce kalibrácie kľúčových meradiel"* and *"nedostatočne vykonaný teplotný monitoring priestorov"* — [ŠÚKL Výročná správa 2024](https://www.sukl.sk/buxus/docs/rozhodnutia/Vyrocna_sprava_2024_final.pdf)
- **Inspection activity is real**: 478 pharmacy and 73 distributor inspections in 2024 — [ŠÚKL Výročná správa 2024](https://www.sukl.sk/buxus/docs/rozhodnutia/Vyrocna_sprava_2024_final.pdf)
- **Incumbent scale mismatch**: Eupry's Enterprise tier targets installations *"exceeding 500 measuring points"* — [Eupry pricing](https://eupry.com/temperature-monitoring/monitoring-price/)
- **Incumbent framing mismatch**: ELPRO's pharmacy page leads on *"FDA 21 CFR Part 11"* and *"GAMP® 5"*, with no Annex 11 or GDP mention in the retrieved content — [ELPRO pharmacy monitoring](https://www.elpro.com/en/pharmacy-temperature-monitoring)
- **Pricing opacity is near-universal**: no published price for validation packages, mapping, or systems from ELPRO, Vaisala, Testo, LogHub or Rotronic; Eupry publishes only *"From €200/yearly"* and *"flexible pricing tailored to your specific requirements"* — [Eupry pricing](https://eupry.com/temperature-monitoring/monitoring-price/); [Eupry mapping pricing](https://eupry.com/temperature-monitoring/mapping-price/); [LogHub.sk](https://www.loghub.sk/)
- **Accreditation need not be owned**: KALIST sells into Slovakia on the basis of **Czech ČIA** accreditation — [KALIST](https://www.kalistakl.sk/kalibracia-teplomerov) — while SNAS accredits Slovak laboratories — [SNAS](https://www.snas.sk/akreditacia/kalibracne-laboratoria)
- **Low input costs**: calibrated loggers €66–€154 retail in Slovakia — [Metershop](https://metershop.sk/kategoria/teplomery/s-certifikatom-o-kalibracii/lekarne-zdravotnictvo/s-automatickym-zaznamom-pre-lekarne/); accredited calibration €19.68–€72.57 per instrument — [Metershop calibration](https://metershop.sk/sluzby/kalibracia-teplomerov/)
- **No Slovak national guidance on temperature monitoring/mapping was found**, in contrast to the Czech SÚKL's DIS-15 v4 — [DIS-15 v4](https://sukl.gov.cz/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/DIS-15_verze4_Sledovani-a-kontrola-teploty-pri-skladovani-a-preprave-leciv.pdf)
- **Counter-evidence — the incumbent local competitor**: LogHub is Slovak, claims 400+ facilities including Hospital Poprad and pharmacy chains, holds ISO 9001 and ISO 27001, offers automatic recalibration by sensor exchange, references ŠÚKL, and already markets *"Nová legislatíva 2026 – elektronické meranie teplôt"* — [LogHub.sk](https://www.loghub.sk/)
- **Counter-evidence — resellers are already in the niche**: Metershop runs a dedicated *"s automatickým záznamom pre lekárne"* category, and KALIST sells *"Kalibrované prístroje pre ordinácie a lekárne"* — [Metershop](https://metershop.sk/kategoria/teplomery/s-certifikatom-o-kalibracii/lekarne-zdravotnictvo/s-automatickym-zaznamom-pre-lekarne/); [KALIST](https://www.kalistakl.sk/kalibrovane-pristroje-pre-ordinacie-a-lekarne)

### Inferences
The defensible gaps, in descending order of strength:
1. **Slovak-decree conformity documentation.** No vendor found publishes a clause-by-clause statement against vyhláška 129/2012 § 30 ods. 8 / § 31 ods. 3. Producing one — in Slovak, citing the § and the 2-hour/12-month tests — is cheap, unique, and directly usable by the customer during an inspection.
2. **Serving the 1–5 sensor customer properly.** Incumbents are built for 500+ points; the independent pharmacy is over-served by enterprise offers and under-served by bare e-shop hardware. A packaged "two sensors, certificates, 12-month archive, monthly Slovak report, recalibration handled" product occupies empty ground between them.
3. **Published, fixed pricing.** In a market where every competitor requires a quote, transparent pricing is both a differentiator and a trust substitute for brand — which matters disproportionately for a sole trader.
4. **Calibration lifecycle as the service, not the hardware.** Since the legal hook is *kalibrované meradlo*, managing certificate expiry and sensor swaps (matching LogHub's exchange model) is the recurring revenue and the real customer pain.
5. **Inspection-ready artefacts.** A monthly Slovak-language report with sign-off and deviation fields matches the vaccine guidance's requirement for name and corrective action, which no generic logger export provides.
6. **Mapping as a second act.** Low equipment cost, no published competitor pricing, and a distributor segment (128/2012) where the rule is vague and advisory credibility sells.

Realistic constraints the report should state alongside the opportunity: LogHub already owns the "Slovak + ŠÚKL + 2026 legislation" position and has references; hardware is commodity and available to anyone through distribution; the compliant hardware floor is under €300 per pharmacy, which caps subscription pricing power; and the sole trader cannot claim accreditation he does not hold. The viable strategy is service and documentation around subcontracted accredited calibration, aimed at independent pharmacies rather than chains, not a hardware or platform play.

### Gaps
- **The size of the addressable market is unknown** — no figure for the number of pharmacies or licensed wholesale distributors in Slovakia was obtained. This is the single most important missing input for sizing the opportunity and should be a priority for follow-up (ŠÚKL registers would be the source).
- LogHub's pricing and packaging are unpublished, so the competitive price point cannot be benchmarked.
- No evidence was found on how many Slovak pharmacies are *already* compliant with automatic recording, i.e. what share of the market is genuinely unserved as of September 2026 — two months after the deadline. **Note the timing: the 1 July 2026 deadline has already passed at the date of this research, so the initial compliance rush may be largely over.** This materially affects the opportunity and was not investigated.
- Sanctions for non-compliance (which would drive urgency) are paywalled and unknown.
- Whether ŠÚKL has begun enforcing the new § 30 ods. 8 requirement in inspections since July 2026 is unknown; no post-deadline enforcement data was found.
